Before you come

Practical notes

  • Bring your passport or ID

    Overseas guests bring a passport, Chinese guests a resident ID card, for registration on site. If you need a letter for a business visa, mention it in your reply.

  • Wear closed-toe shoes

    Flat, closed-toe shoes and long trousers are best on the production floor. We provide the protective equipment you need, such as a hard hat and safety glasses.

  • Safety on the floor

    Every visit starts with a short safety briefing. On the production floor, stay with your host and keep to the marked walkways.

  • Photography

    Photos are welcome in reception and meeting areas. Please ask your host before taking photos on the production floor or in the lab.

  • Interpreter available

    An interpreter can join your visit. Tick the box in the form below and tell us your preferred language.

  • Address and route

    We send the exact address, directions and the day's schedule when we confirm your visit.
